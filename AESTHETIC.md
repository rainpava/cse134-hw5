For my aesthetic, I went with a neon/cyber style aesthetic (https://colorany.com/color-palettes/cyber-color-palettes/). My CSS expresses this by contrasting plain solid-color backgrounds with a bright, saturated, color to contrast it. The JavaScript Enhancement I made overlaps with the Theme picker from HW5. I preserved a11y by adding the aria-pressed attribute to the theme buttons so that a11y tools could better read the site. This site ties into the "medium is the message" idea because the aesthetic I chose to represent also represents things about me beyond visual preferences, for example, this cyber aesthetic could indicate that I'm very interested in the idea of the future and what comes with it, as well as highlighting my appreciation for technology, which ties into my computer science major.
